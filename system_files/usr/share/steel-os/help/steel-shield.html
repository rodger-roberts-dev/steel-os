Steel Shield

Built-in, free virus protection. No subscription, no ads, nothing to sign up for.

What it does by itself

  • Downloads: every file that lands in Downloads is checked within seconds.
  • USB drives: when you plug one in, a notification asks Scan this drive? Programs and documents are checked first.
  • Weekly: your home folder is checked on Tuesday afternoon, at the lowest priority. It waits while you're playing a game.
  • Definitions: the list of known threats updates every hour, plus a daily list of brand-new threats from abuse.ch.

If it finds something

The file is moved to quarantine, where it can't run. Nothing is ever deleted without you choosing to. A notification says what it was, in plain English, with Restore and Details buttons.

Trust the file (for example a game mod or a tool you know)? Click Restore, or in Steel Shield choose Quarantine, the file, then Restore it and always allow this file.

Behaviour watching

Virus definitions only know threats that have already been seen. Steel Shield also watches what programs do, the way a Mac or Windows does:

  • Something sets itself to start when you log in. If it's an app from the App Store or part of the system, you get a quiet note. Anything else asks: Keep or Remove. Removed items are kept aside, not deleted.
  • A program changes lots of your files at once. If a program from Downloads, a temporary folder or a script suddenly rewrites or renames many files in Documents, Desktop, Pictures, Music or Videos, the way ransomware does, Steel Shield pauses it and asks: Stop it or Allow it.
  • A hidden program works the processor hard. A program from Downloads or a temporary folder using the processor heavily for minutes, the way hidden cryptocurrency miners do, is paused and you're asked the same. Games are never touched.
  • A downloaded file is made runnable. Steel Shield checks it first and reminds you it came from the internet.

Programs that come with Steel OS and apps from the App Store are never paused. Allow it remembers your choice. To switch behaviour watching off, open Steel Shield and choose Turn behaviour watching off.

Block dangerous websites (optional)

In Steel Shield, Block dangerous websites for every app makes the whole computer look websites up through Quad9, a free non-profit service that refuses known malware and phishing sites. It asks for your password once. It's off until you turn it on.

Open Steel Shield

Start menu, Steel Shield, or the Steel OS Portal, Welcome, Steel Shield. It shows whether you are protected and lets you:

  • Scan now (your home folder) or Scan a file or folder.
  • See Quarantine: restore or delete items.
  • See the History of what was found.
  • Turn download scanning on or off.

Right-click any file or folder in Dolphin and choose Scan with Steel Shield.

"Needs attention"

Steel Shield never shows a green tick when something is wrong. If the definitions are old or a part has stopped, it says so and offers Fix now.

Check that it works

Download the harmless EICAR test file from eicar.org. Within seconds Steel Shield moves it to quarantine and tells you. Then delete it from Quarantine.

Steel Shield uses ClamAV with definitions from Cisco Talos. It is strongest against known threats; Steel OS's read-only system, signed updates and app sandboxes protect against the rest.
