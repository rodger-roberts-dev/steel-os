Getting started

If you have used Windows, you already know most of Steel OS.

The desktop

  • Start menu: the Steel OS button at the bottom left, or press the Super key (the Windows key). Start typing to search for any app, file or setting.
  • Taskbar: along the bottom. Next to the start button: Firefox, the Steel OS Portal, the file manager, the App Store, Steel Shield, Steam, Lutris and the terminal, then your open windows. On the right: the desktops 1 to 4 (Super+1 to 4), Wi-Fi, sound, notifications and the clock.
  • Add or remove taskbar icons: right-click an app in the start menu and choose Pin to Task Manager. To remove one, right-click it on the taskbar and choose Unpin from Task Manager. Drag icons to change their order.
  • Hide the taskbar: press Super+T to switch between always visible and auto-hide (it slides back when the mouse touches the bottom edge). The start menu also has Auto-hide the taskbar and Always show the taskbar: right-click Show or Auto-hide the Taskbar.
  • Virtual desktops: Steel OS starts with four, shown as the boxes 1 to 4 next to the clock (Super+1 to 4 switch between them). Don't use them? Press Super+Shift+D to switch them off: one desktop, and the boxes leave the taskbar. Press it again to bring them back. The start menu has Virtual Desktops On or Off too.
  • Windows open on top of each other, like Windows. Drag them by the title bar; the buttons at the top right minimise, maximise and close.
  • Four desktops: extra screens to spread your windows across. Press Super+1 to 4 to switch.

The Steel OS Portal

The Portal is the orange S in a hexagon, next to Firefox on the taskbar. It has:

  • Welcome: the website, the App Store, keyboard shortcuts, Update now and Report a problem or idea.
  • Steel Apps: popular apps by category, one click each.
  • More Apps and Tools, Media Apps, Manage Steel OS, Tweak Systems, Troubleshoot: extra tools for when you need them.

Settings

Open System Settings from the start menu for Wi-Fi, displays, sound, Bluetooth, printers, users and the look of the desktop.

First-time setup

The welcome steps you saw at first login can be run again any time: start menu, Steel OS Setup.
